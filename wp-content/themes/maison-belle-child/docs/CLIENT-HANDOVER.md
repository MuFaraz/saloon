# Your new website: a one-page guide

*For the salon owner. No technical knowledge needed.*

## Signing in
Go to **your-website.com/wp-admin** and sign in with the username and password you were given. Everything below is in the black menu on the left.

## The five things you will do most

| I want to… | Click… | Then… |
|---|---|---|
| Change phone, address, hours, booking link, social links | **Salon Settings** | Pick the tab, change the text, press **Save Changes** |
| Add or change a price | **Services** | Open the service (or **Add New**), change **Price**, press **Update** |
| Add photos to the gallery | **Gallery → Add New** | Type a short description, set the **Photo** on the right, choose a category, **Publish** |
| Add a client review | **Reviews → Add New** | Client name as title, type the review, **Publish** |
| Write a blog article | **Posts → Add New** | Write, add a **Featured image** on the right, **Publish** |

## Everything else

* **Special offers**: *Promotions → Add New* (e.g. "15% off"). To stop an offer, move it to Trash.
* **Your logo and brand colours**: logo in *Salon Settings → General*. Colours: *Appearance → Customize → Salon colours & fonts*.
* **The big photos on the home page**: *Salon Settings → Home page*. Until you add photos, soft coloured placeholders are shown. Nothing breaks if you add them later.
* **Opening hours**: *Salon Settings → Contact, hours & booking*. Fill the rows: "Mon – Sat" and "10am – 7pm". Tick the days so Google shows the right hours.
* **Where booking buttons go**: paste your booking page web address in *Salon Settings → Contact, hours & booking → Booking page URL*. Every *Book Now* button on the site uses it.
* **Messages from the contact form** arrive at the email address in *Salon Settings → Contact, hours & booking → Send contact form messages to*. Please send yourself a test message after any change.
* **Policy page**: *Pages → Policy*. Each big heading becomes a numbered section.

## Photo tips
* Use bright, sharp photos taken in daylight; upload them as JPG, ideally 1600 pixels wide.
* Always fill in the short description when asked ("Soft pink gel manicure"). It helps visitors who use screen readers and helps Google.
* Portrait (tall) photos suit the home page and services; any shape works in the gallery.

## Please don't
* Don't click **Update** on WordPress, themes or plugins yourself unless your web person says so. A backup is taken first when they do it.
* Don't delete the "Maison Belle Core" or "Maison Belle Child" items: they hold your content and design.
* Don't share your password. Change it under *Users → Profile*.

## If something looks wrong
Take a screenshot, note what page it was on and what you clicked, and send it to your web person. Backups are taken regularly, so nothing can be permanently lost.

## Checklist before you go live
- [ ] My real phone, address, email and hours are in Salon Settings
- [ ] My booking link is in Salon Settings and opens the right page
- [ ] My own photos replaced the soft placeholders
- [ ] The sample reviews, offers and blog posts were replaced with real ones
- [ ] I sent a test message through the Contact page and received it
- [ ] I read the Policy page and it says what I want
